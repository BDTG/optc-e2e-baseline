Notes

  • WSL2 on teacher machine was auto-restarting every ~10min during testing (Windows stayed up; only the WSL VM cycled). Workaround: keep a terminal open inside WSL to prevent idle shutdown. Patches persist on disk.
  • Full 8-epoch training projected: 66×8=528s → 40×8=320s (save ~3.5 min/run)
  • The prefetch thread eliminates GPU idle-during-disk-read; further gains would require parallelizing msg extraction (CPU-bound section).
